4 · Rules of thumb

Background is always warm cream #FBF6ED — never pure white pages (white is for cards).
Dashed borders (#E3D6BF) = section dividers; solid (#EEE3CE) = card edges.
Prices always Bricolage 800 in terracotta #B4552D with ₹ and Indian comma format.
Every listing shows distance before anything else — proximity is the product.
Seller notes are italic, quoted, first-person. Never marketing copy.
Playful rotation (-6° logo tile, ±1–3° hovers) is a garnish: max 1–2 rotated elements per view.
Emoji are the icon set (🏠 💬 📍 ♡) — no icon font.
Hit targets ≥ 44px on mobile · body text ≥ 13px.
Motion: bob (float), pulse (you-dot), pop (.25–.35s ease-out) for new content. Nothing else.
